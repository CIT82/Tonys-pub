Momo's Profile --

The Dancing Queen of TWICE

Hirai Momo was born on November 9, 1996, in Kyotanabe, Kyoto, Japan. Recognized globally as one of the best dancers in the K-pop industry, Momo's sharp execution, incredible agility, and powerful stage performances make her a cornerstone of TWICE's choreography. She joined JYP Entertainment as a trainee in 2012, participated in the survival show SIXTEEN and debuted with TWICE in 2015.

  • Nationality: Japanese
  • Official Color: Soft Pink
  • Hobbies: Dancing, Watching Movies, Playing Video Games
