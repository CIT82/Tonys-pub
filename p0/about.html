About TWICE

TWICE is a South Korean girl group formed by JYP Entertainment. The group is composed of nine members: Nayeon, Jeongyeon, Momo, Sana, Jihyo, Mina, Dahyun, Chaeyoung, and Tzuyu. They debuted on October 20, 2015, with the extended play (EP) "The Story Begins". TWICE has gained immense popularity in South Korea and internationally for their catchy songs and energetic performances.
