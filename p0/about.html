About TWICE

TWICE is a South Korean girl group formed by JYP Entertainment. The group is composed of nine members: Nayeon, Jeongyeon, Momo, Sana, Jihyo, Mina, Dahyun, Chaeyoung, and Tzuyu. They debuted on October 20, 2015, with the extended play (EP) "The Story Begins". TWICE has gained immense popularity in South Korea and internationally for their catchy songs and energetic performances.

TWICE's Journey

From Survival Roots to Global Superstardom

TWICE's incredible journey began in 2015 through the intense Mnet survival reality show SIXTEEN, where JYP Entertainment trainees competed fiercely for a spot in the final lineup. Out of the contestants, the final nine members Nayeon, Jeongyeon, Momo, Sana, Jihyo, Mina, Dahyun, Chaeyoung, and Tzuyu were selected to form the powerhouse group we know today.

On October 20, 2015, they officially burst onto the K-pop scene with their debut EP The Story Begins and the wildly catchy title track "Like OOH-AHH". Their distinct color, vibrant sound, and infectious choreography quickly captured public attention, setting the stage for a historic run of consecutive chart-topping hits like "Cheer Up", "TT", and "Signal".

Over the years, TWICE evolved from a dominant digital chart force in Asia into a global stadium-selling phenomenon, breaking records for girl group album sales and selling out massive world tours across North America, Europe, and beyond. Alongside their monumental group success, the members have expanded their artistry through creative solo debuts, unit projects like MISAMO, and ventures into acting and fashion, proving that their bond as nine remains stronger than ever as they continue to write music for ONCE.
