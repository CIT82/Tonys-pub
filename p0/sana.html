Sana's Profile -

Our Cute and lovable Hamster Princess

Minatozaki Sana was born on December 29, 1996, in Tennōji-ku, Osaka, Japan. Renowned for her captivating expressions, bright charisma, and iconic viral killing parts (such as 'Shy Shy Shy'), Sana brings sparkling energy to every TWICE release. She debuted in October 2015 after being scouted while shopping with a friend in Japan.
