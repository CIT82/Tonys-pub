Jihyo's Profile

Park Jihyo was born on February 1, 1997, in Guri, Gyeonggi-do, South Korea. As the fearless leader and powerhouse main vocalist of TWICE, Jihyo trained for over ten years under JYP Entertainment before her debut. In August 2023, she made her stellar solo debut with her mini-album Zone and title track 'Killin' Me Good'.
